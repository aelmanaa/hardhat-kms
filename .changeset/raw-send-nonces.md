Keep a library account's sends and the plugin's own sends on distinct nonces. A `connection.kms.getAccount` account now has a viem `nonceManager`. For each send without a nonce, viem asks it for one: it reads the node's pending count through the connection, unchanged, and skips the nonces the plugin used or reserved, under the account's send lock. With a client whose transport is `custom(connection.provider)`, the send then keeps that lock until its `eth_sendRawTransaction` (or `eth_sendRawTransactionSync`, from `sendTransactionSync` and `writeContractSync`) goes out or viem resets it after a failure, as a send through the plugin keeps it from fill to broadcast. That raw transaction raises the nonce high-water mark. A client with its own transport, such as `http(url)`, releases the lock at once and its nonce is reserved: sends through the plugin skip it until viem resets it or 60 s pass, and a warning says the broadcast is not ordered. When one account uses clients of both kinds at once, a `reset` ends a reservation before it ends a held send's lock, since viem's `reset` does not say which send failed. No RPC answer is rewritten, and raw transactions from other senders, or that the plugin cannot decode (such as a blob transaction with its sidecars), pass on untouched. A library send started from inside a send from the same account fails at once with `core.account.nonce-reentrant`. A send that waits more than 5 s behind a library send's hold prints one warning, and so does a hold that its 60 s limit ends. On an automining node, a plugin send can be refused with "Nonce too high" while a client with its own transport signs or broadcasts the reserved nonce; send through `custom(connection.provider)` or wait for that send's receipt. The viem peer range is now `^2.55.13`, the first release that calls the nonce manager's `reset` after any failed send and only after a `consume`. `getAccount` refuses an older viem before any KMS call, since pnpm and Yarn install it with only a peer warning. The warning printed on the first transaction a library account signs is gone. If your project pins viem to an exact version below 2.55.13, such as `"viem": "2.47.6"`, bump it to 2.55.13 or later: npm can stop the install with a peer dependency conflict (`ERESOLVE`).
